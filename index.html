Introduction

Texas has endured persistent, record-breaking heat throughout much of 2026, placing it among 18 U.S. states on pace for their hottest year on record according to NOAA data.


Extreme heat waves gripped the region across the summer months, pushing triple-digit highs in North Texas well into late August and September.


Deep South Texas was hit especially hard, with cities including Brownsville, Harlingen, and McAllen recording their hottest September in history.
